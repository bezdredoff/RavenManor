# Story Journal Visuals

The journal is a readable prototype archive rather than a decorative book
simulation.

## Entry states

- **Locked** — grayscale, reduced opacity, level condition only.
- **New** — wine border and prominent `Смотреть` action.
- **Viewed** — neutral card with `Пересмотреть`.
- **Major** — subtle gold badge after unlock.

## Mobile layout

- one vertical list grouped into five room sections;
- no sticky header;
- controls remain at least 44 px where space permits;
- at narrow or short viewports, entry actions move to a full-width second row;
- scrolling stays inside the application frame.
