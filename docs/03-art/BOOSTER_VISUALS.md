# Booster visuals

The prototype uses authored transparent SVG assets so boosters remain readable
at 28–42 px on a phone.

## Silver Hammer

- pale engraved steel head;
- dark rosewood handle;
- warm gold magical spark;
- silhouette must remain distinct from match-3 tiles.

File: `src/assets/boosters/hammer.svg`

## Raven Shuffle

- two crossing silver-violet paths;
- mirrored arrowheads;
- small gold raven-wing marks at the centre;
- communicates rearrangement without using a generic emoji.

File: `src/assets/boosters/shuffle.svg`

Both assets are prototype-quality and intentionally match the existing romantic
gothic UI. Final production art may replace them without changing booster IDs
or gameplay logic.
