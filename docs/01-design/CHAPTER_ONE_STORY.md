# Chapter One Story — 30-scene contract

FEATURE-052 gives every one of the 30 chapter levels an authored story event.

## Cadence

- levels 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, and 30 are major scenes;
- the other twenty levels use shorter interludes;
- every room contains six scenes and two major turning points;
- completing a level unlocks its scene immediately;
- the story remains optional at the result screen and can be read later.

## Room arcs

1. **Hall, 1–6** — Evelyn returns, meets Adrian, and proves that her childhood
   was erased.
2. **Library, 7–12** — the Night Circle contract and Evelyn's own memory-erasure
   order are discovered.
3. **Garden, 13–18** — Lucian's name, the failed escape, and the childhood oath
   return.
4. **Crypt, 19–24** — the substitution sacrifice, Evelyn's false grave, and
   Adrian's confession are revealed.
5. **Tower, 25–30** — Evelyn remembers the stolen night, opens the tower, and
   releases Lucian while a deeper contract awakens.

## Spoiler policy

Locked journal entries show only their level number and unlock condition. Titles
and summaries appear only after the corresponding level is completed.

Lucian keeps the silhouette portrait through level 29. His authored portrait is
used only in the final scene after level 30.
