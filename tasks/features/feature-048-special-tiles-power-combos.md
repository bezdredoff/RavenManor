# FEATURE-048 — Special Tiles and Power Combos

## Goal

Reward advanced match shapes and increase tactical variety without changing
level content, saves, blockers, or boosters.

## Included

- line-4 row/column rocket;
- T/L explosive rune;
- square `2×2` ghost raven;
- line-5 lunar prism;
- rocket+rocket, rocket+rune, rune+rune, prism+normal;
- recursive chain activation;
- objective-aware Raven targeting;
- shared live/Hint resolution logic;
- reshuffle/collapse support;
- authored SVGs, procedural cues, UI text, analytics, docs, and tests.

## Excluded

- blocker damage;
- boosters/inventory;
- mechanic unlocks through rooms;
- Raven pair combinations;
- Prism + special;
- special creation during cascades;
- balance/content expansion beyond the existing ten levels.
