# FEATURE-052 — Story journal and complete first chapter

## Goal

Make the 30-level chapter narratively complete and let players revisit every
unlocked story event without changing progression.

## Acceptance criteria

- exactly one authored scene exists for every level 1–30;
- ten scenes are major and twenty are interludes;
- journal entries are grouped into five six-scene room arcs;
- locked, new, and viewed states are derived from existing progress;
- locked entries hide spoilers and show their unlock level;
- Home and Manor link to the journal;
- replay returns to the journal and does not mutate gameplay progress;
- first views and replays are analytically distinct;
- Lucian's revealed portrait appears only after level 30;
- source, all tests, SVG assets, and the production build validate.
