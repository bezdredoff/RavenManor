# FEATURE-050 — Connected meta progression and boosters

## Goal

Make restoration the visible reason to play match-3 and introduce two reusable
non-monetised prototype boosters.

## Acceptance criteria

- The level map displays the current progression-critical restoration task.
- Level groups 4–6, 7–9 and 10 unlock from configured restoration tasks.
- Existing saves never lose access to groups they already entered.
- The win modal offers an affordable repair as a primary action.
- Restoration tasks communicate level/mechanic unlocks and booster rewards.
- Silver Hammer removes one tile or obstacle layer without spending a move.
- Shuffle rearranges movable tiles without spending a move.
- Booster inventory persists, exports, imports and migrates safely.
- Optional third room tasks grant larger completion rewards.
- Mobile gameplay remains viewport-locked.
