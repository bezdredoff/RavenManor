# FEATURE-051 — Thirty-level chapter

## Goal

Expand the playable chapter from 10 to 30 data-driven levels while preserving
save compatibility, repair gates, mobile layout, offline delivery, and existing
gameplay systems.

## Acceptance criteria

- exactly 30 validated levels;
- five six-level room arcs;
- ten three-level progression groups;
- restoration gates open every group after the first;
- room finales at 6, 12, 18, 24, and 30;
- existing saves retain completed levels and replay access;
- only milestone levels show the current ten story scenes;
- no level-specific engine branches;
- all levels repeatedly generate with a legal opening move;
- documentation and public playtest checklist updated.
