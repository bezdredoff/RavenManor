# FEATURE-053 — Localization, journal notification, and global settings

## Goal

Make story discovery clearer, reorganize Settings, support Russian/English/
Belarusian, and make Settings reachable without losing the caller screen.

## Acceptance criteria

- Home uses only the Journal for story access.
- Unread unlocked scenes add a localized `New` badge and optional animation.
- Audio settings appear before every other settings category.
- The contextual mechanics note follows the hints/board guidance.
- RU, EN, and BE cover interface, levels, rooms, restoration, and all 30 scenes.
- Locale persists under `ravenManorLocaleV1` without changing game progress.
- A gear appears beside stars on all primary screens.
- Back from Settings restores the caller and preserves active Match-3 state.
- Reduced Motion disables the journal attention animation.
